Note 16 Rebuilding the Corpus

Construction of the 49.8 million pair training corpus: 5.05M augmented SwissProt rows across 4 tiers (original, dropout variants, LLM natural language, minimal) plus 44.8M enriched Pfam rows with BLAST-transferred annotations, corrected lineages, and enzyme classifications.

Note 17 Sector Architecture Across Pfam

Automated SCA decomposition of ~20,000 Pfam-A families into independent components. Why ICs (not merged sectors) are the training signal. Why connectivity (not phylogeny) defines the classification. Two-pass architecture from MSA to PDB-numbered annotations.

19,578 families 816K IC positions 4 validation families ℒ_sector loss

Note 18 PenCL on Aurora

Porting PenCL contrastive pretraining from NVIDIA CUDA to Intel XPU on the Aurora exascale system. A complete catalog of 11 fixes — from pynvml imports to bf16 infinity masking to XCCL distributed initialization — with the technical detail needed to reproduce each solution. Covers the full path from single-tile confirmation to 12-rank scaling, the OOM memory wall, and rank-aware data splitting.
