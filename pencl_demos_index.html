PenCL Demos

Interactive demonstrations from the PenCL retraining project — rebuilding the text-protein embedding space for natural language protein design. PenCL was trained on 45 million text-protein pairs in a single annotation format. These demos document the corpus reconstruction, sector architecture analysis, and retraining that make the embedding space format-invariant.

Demo 1 Tier 3 — Natural Language Protein Descriptions

100 SwissProt proteins described in genuinely diverse scientific vocabulary by Claude Sonnet. Each protein is shown with its original database annotation alongside two LLM-generated descriptions — one from a molecular/cellular perspective, one from a structural/evolutionary perspective — demonstrating the vocabulary shift that teaches PenCL format invariance.

Demo 2 Pfam-wide SCA — Sector Architecture Across 20,000 Families

Automated decomposition of every Pfam-A family into independent components using statistical coupling analysis. Validation on S1A serine proteases, chorismate mutases, SH3 domains, and AMP-binding enzymes. Physical connectivity distinguishes sectors (cooperative functional cores) from co-sectors (tunable structural shells).

Does training on a format-diverse 49.8M pair corpus fix the vocabulary memorization problem? Comparing the embedding space of the retrained PenCL (Run 1) against the original: within-protein format consistency, inter-family separation, and cross-format retrieval.
